Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026

The game does not collect any personally identifiable information from users. Your gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. Apart from the advertising described below, your data is not stored or transmitted to any servers.

Game Center

The app uses Apple’s Game Center to allow you to track your scores and achievements, and compete on leaderboards. All data handled by Game Center is managed by Apple, and we do not store or access any personal Game Center data.

Third-Party Content

Apart from the Google AdMob ads described below, this app does not access or display third-party content other than Apple Game Center features integrated via official APIs. All necessary rights for such integrations are covered under Apple's developer agreements.

Advertising

Some of our apps, including Melt It!, show ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, an approximate location based on your IP address, information about the ads you see and tap, and basic crash and performance data. We don't collect or store this information ourselves, and we don't use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy. You can limit ad personalisation at any time in your device's settings under Privacy & Security.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment details.

Everything Else

Our apps don't ask for an account, and they don't collect any other personal information. Game progress is stored on your device.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
